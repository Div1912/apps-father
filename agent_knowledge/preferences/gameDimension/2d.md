DIMENSION = 2D.

- Use `THREE.OrthographicCamera`. Compute frustum from viewport: `const aspect = w/h; const viewSize = TARGET_VIEW_HEIGHT;` then `left = -viewSize*aspect/2; right = viewSize*aspect/2; top = viewSize/2; bottom = -viewSize/2;`. `near = 0.1`, `far = 1000`.
- Pick `TARGET_VIEW_HEIGHT` to match the gameplay area in world units (e.g. a runner with 12 visible lanes uses `viewSize = 12`).
- Camera position: looks straight down (`(0, 50, 0)` looking at `(0,0,0)`) for top-down, or straight along Z (`(0, 0, 50)` looking at `(0,0,0)`) for side-scroll.
- Geometry: `THREE.PlaneGeometry` quads with textures, OR `THREE.BoxGeometry` with negligible depth — both work, planes are cheaper.
- No perspective foreshortening tricks. Parallax is faked by moving multiple camera-fixed layers at different speeds.
- Shadows OFF (orthographic shadows on flat geometry add cost without payoff). Use a single `THREE.AmbientLight` at full intensity.
- Isometric look in 2D = orthographic camera tilted to `(20, 30, 20)` looking at origin. State this explicitly in the plan if used.
- Background: solid color clear, OR a single full-screen quad behind everything.
