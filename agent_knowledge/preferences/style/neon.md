STYLE = Neon.

- Palette: background `#08070f`, surface `#15122b`, text `#f8fafc`, muted `#a3a8c3`, primary `#7c3aed` (white text), accent `#22d3ee`, border `#2a2350`.
- Typography: Orbitron for headings/display, Inter for body. Use uppercase tracking on primary CTAs.
- Geometry: 16px corner radius, glow shadow `0 0 24px rgba(124,58,237,0.45)` on primary CTAs and active tabs.
- Effects: subtle gradient borders are allowed (`conic-gradient(...)` or `linear-gradient(...)` masked with `padding-box`). Never use pure white surfaces. Primary buttons must have a visible glow.
- Backdrops: glass surfaces with `backdrop-filter: blur(18px)` over translucent `rgba(124,58,237,0.08)`.
