STYLE = Titanium.

- Palette: background `#111111`, surface `#1a1a1a`, text gradient `linear-gradient(160deg, #fff 0%, #aaa 60%, #555 100%)` (use `background-clip: text`), muted `#333`, accent `#4ade80` (live/positive only), border `#1e1e1e`.
- Typography: Space Grotesk. Hero numbers: 40–48px, weight 700, letter-spacing -3px, gradient fill white→dark for metallic feel.
- Special: 6px tall "anodized" top bar with multi-stop linear-gradient `#1a1a1a → #888 (mid) → #1a1a1a` to imitate brushed metal.
- Geometry: 12–20px corner radius, hairline 1px borders in `#1e1e1e/#333`. Dividers use gradient-fade lines (transparent→`#3a3a3a`→transparent) for metallic highlight.
- Inset highlight: every surface and button gets `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` for a convex/embossed feel, plus `0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)` for depth.
- Tone: premium without gold. Titanium card energy. Fintech, portfolios, status apps.
- Forbidden: bright accents (besides the single mint-green live indicator), neon glow, glassmorphism, warm tones.
