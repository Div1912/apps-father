What is MusioGram

AI music that
doesn't exist anywhere else

MusioGram is a Telegram Mini App music streaming platform powered entirely by AI-generated tracks. Every song is unique — you won't find these anywhere on the internet.

Users get 25-second previews for free. To unlock a full track, they pay 1 Telegram Star. Or subscribe to Premium to get everything — all tracks, shuffle, new releases, and AI art.

The creator built a complete, monetized, working product with Apps Father for a total investment of just $100 — and the platform is already live and earning.
