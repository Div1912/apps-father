# Apps Father — User Guide

Build Telegram Mini Apps without writing a single line of code. Just describe your idea, and Apps Father turns it into a fully working app inside Telegram.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Open the Bot

Find **@AppsFatherBot** in Telegram and tap **Start**. You'll see a welcome screen with your balance and the main menu.

### 2. Main Menu

From the main menu you can:

- **Create App** — Start a new project
- **App List** — View and manage your existing apps
- **Top Up** — Add funds to your balance
- **Help** — Quick overview of how everything works

You can always return to the main menu by tapping **Main Menu** on the bottom keyboard.

---

## Creating Your First App

### Step 1: Create a Bot

Tap **Create App & Bot**. Telegram will prompt you to create a new bot — this is the bot that will host your Mini App. Give it a name and confirm.

### Step 2: Describe Your App

Once the bot is created, you'll see your new project in the app list. Tap it and then tap **Describe Your App**.

Tell the AI what you want to build. Be as detailed as you like:

- What features should it have?
- What's the main purpose?
- Any design preferences (colors, layout, style)?
- Who is the target audience?

**Tip:** You can also send images along with your description as design references — screenshots, mockups, or any visual inspiration.

### Step 3: Review the Plan

The AI will generate a detailed plan for your app. You'll see:

- What pages and features will be built
- How the app will be structured
- Technical approach

You have three options:

- **Approve & Build** — The AI starts building immediately
- **Modify** — Tell the AI what to change in the plan
- **Decline** — Start over with a new description

### Step 4: Wait for the Build

Once approved, the AI agent goes to work. You'll see a live progress bar showing what it's doing — creating files, writing code, setting up the backend. This usually takes 1–3 minutes depending on complexity.

### Step 5: Launch Your App

When the build is complete, you'll see a summary of what was built and the app dashboard buttons. Tap **Open App** to launch it through your project's bot.

## App Dashboard

After your app is built, the project dashboard gives you these options:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Open App** | **Update App** |
| **Admin Panel** *(if purchased)* | **Edit Code** *(if purchased)* |
| **Release Version** | **Suggestions** |
| **Features** | |
| **Remove** | **Back** |

### Open App

Opens your Mini App through the project's bot. Share the bot link with anyone to let them use your app.

### Update App

Request changes to your app — add features, fix bugs, change the design, or make any modifications. Just describe what you want in plain text.

You can also **attach photos and files** with your update message. For example:
- Send a screenshot of a design you want to replicate
- Attach an image or icon to use inside the app
- Send multiple files at once — they'll all be saved into your project

Files are automatically saved to your project's assets folder and the AI will use them when applying your update.

### Release Version

Save a snapshot of your current app as a named version (e.g., "1.0.0", "beta", "v2"). This helps you track progress over time.

### Suggestions

The AI analyzes your app and proposes improvements — better features, UI enhancements, performance tweaks. You can apply any suggestion with one tap, or skip them all.

### Remove

Permanently deletes the project, including all files, data, and bot configuration. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is deleted.

## Premium Features

Each project has a **Features** store where you can unlock additional capabilities. Tap **Features** on your project dashboard to browse.

| Feature | Price | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Stars Payment System** | $15 | Enables Telegram Stars payment integration in your app |
| **TON Payment System** | $25 | Enables TON blockchain payment integration in your app |
| **Admin Panel** | $30 | Unlocks an Admin Panel for managing your app's data |
| **Disable Splash** | $10 | Removes the "Made by Apps Father" splash screen |
| **Get Code** | $200 | Access and edit the full source code of your app |

### How to Purchase

1. Go to your project dashboard and tap **Features**
2. Tap the feature you want — you'll see a description and price
3. If you have enough balance, tap **Confirm Purchase**
4. The feature is unlocked instantly

Purchased features show a ✅ checkmark. Locked features show a 🔒 with the price.

**Note:** The Stars Payment and TON Payment features control whether the AI can implement payment logic in your app. Without purchasing them, payment-related requests will not be fulfilled.

## Balance & Payments

### How Billing Works

Every AI operation costs tokens. When the AI builds your app, generates suggestions, or applies updates, the token usage is calculated and deducted from your balance. You'll see the exact cost after each operation.

### Topping Up

1. Tap **Top Up** from the main menu
2. Enter the amount in USD (minimum $10)
3. You'll receive a payment link via NOWPayments
4. Pay with any supported cryptocurrency
5. Your balance is credited automatically after blockchain confirmation

### Checking Your Balance

Your current balance is always displayed on the main menu screen.

## Tips for Best Results

### Writing Good Descriptions

The more specific your description, the better the result:

- **Good:** "A task manager app with categories, due dates, priority levels, and a dark theme. Users can create tasks, mark them complete, and filter by category."
- **Not as good:** "Make me a task app"

### Sending Design References

When describing your app or requesting an update, attach screenshots or mockups. The AI uses these as visual references to match the design you have in mind.

### Using Suggestions

After your app is built, tap **Suggestions** to get AI-powered improvement ideas. This is a great way to discover features you might not have thought of.

### Iterative Updates

You don't need to get everything right in the first build. Start with the core features, test the app, then use **Update App** to add more features, polish the design, or fix issues step by step.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does it take to build an app?**
A: Most apps are built in 1–3 minutes. Complex apps with many features may take a bit longer.

**Q: Can I update my app after it's built?**
A: Yes, as many times as you want. Each update is a separate AI operation and costs tokens from your balance.

**Q: What kind of apps can I build?**
A: Any Telegram Mini App — booking tools, games, dashboards, voting systems, loyalty programs, stores, calculators, and more. The AI handles frontend, backend, and database.

**Q: Can I share my app with others?**
A: Yes. Share your project bot's username (e.g., @YourAppBot) with anyone. They can open the Mini App directly from the bot.

**Q: What happens if I run out of balance?**
A: You'll be prompted to top up before any AI operation. No work is done until you have sufficient funds.

**Q: Can I get the source code?**
A: Yes, by purchasing the **Get Code** feature ($200) for your project. This unlocks a web-based code editor where you can view and modify all project files.

**Q: Can my app have payments?**
A: Yes. Purchase the **Stars Payment System** ($15) or **TON Payment System** ($25) feature for your project, then ask the AI to implement payment functionality.

**Q: How do I remove the "Made by Apps Father" branding?**
A: Purchase the **Disable Splash** feature ($10) for your project. The splash screen will be removed immediately.
