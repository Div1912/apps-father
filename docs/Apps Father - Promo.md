## What is Apps Father?

Apps Father is a Telegram bot that builds fully functional Mini Apps for you — powered by AI. No developers, no frameworks, no terminal commands. Just describe what you want, and get a working app in minutes.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Describe your idea
Tell the bot what you want — a booking system, a game, a store, a dashboard. Use plain language. Attach screenshots or mockups if you have them.

### 2. Review the plan
The AI generates a structured plan for your app. Approve it, tweak it, or start fresh — you're always in control.

### 3. Get a working app
In 1–3 minutes, your Mini App is live inside Telegram. Frontend, backend, database — all handled automatically.

### 4. Iterate and improve
Request updates anytime. Add features, fix things, change the design. Or tap Suggestions and let the AI propose improvements for you.

## Why Apps Father?

### No code required
Describe your app in words. The AI writes every line for you — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, backend logic, and database.

### Full-stack apps
Not just a pretty page. Your app gets a real backend with API routes and a database for storing and managing data.

### Built-in bot
Every project comes with its own Telegram bot. Users open your Mini App directly from the bot — no extra setup.

### Live updates
Want to change something? Just send a message. The AI reads your existing code, makes the changes, and redeploys — all in one step.

### Visual references
Send photos, screenshots, or design mockups along with your request. The AI uses them to match the look and feel you want.

### AI-powered suggestions
Not sure what to add next? The AI analyzes your app and proposes features, UX improvements, and optimizations.

### Version control
Save named versions of your app (v1.0, beta, v2) so you always have a snapshot of your progress.
